Company Knowledge Base — Account Management

Administrators can invite users from the account administration area. Each user should receive only the permissions necessary for their role.

When an employee leaves a team, administrators should deactivate the user's access promptly. Shared credentials should not be used as a substitute for individual accounts.

Organizations should periodically review user roles and remove access that is no longer required.

If an administrator cannot access the account-management area, they should contact support through the approved support channel and provide the organization's account identifier without sharing passwords or authentication codes.
