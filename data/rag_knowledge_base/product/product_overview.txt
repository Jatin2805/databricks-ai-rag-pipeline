Company Knowledge Base — Product Overview

The company's primary product is a cloud-based analytics platform designed for teams that need dashboards, data exploration, and automated reporting.

The platform supports role-based access control. Administrators can create workspaces, invite users, assign roles, and control access to shared resources.

Users can create dashboards from connected data sources and schedule reports for authorized recipients. Available features can depend on the customer's subscription plan.

The platform is designed for browser-based use. Customers should consult the current product documentation for supported browsers, integrations, and feature availability because these can change over time.
