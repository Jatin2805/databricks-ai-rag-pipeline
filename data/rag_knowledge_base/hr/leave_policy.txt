Company Knowledge Base — Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave should normally be requested at least 3 working days before the intended start date. Managers may approve urgent requests with less notice when business coverage is available.

Up to 10 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the following calendar year. Any carried-forward balance must be used by March 31. Public holidays are not deducted from an employee's annual-leave balance.

Employees who are absent because of illness should follow the company's sick-leave reporting process and notify their manager as soon as practical. Annual leave should not be used to replace a required sick-leave record.

For questions about individual balances, employees should contact HR because balances can depend on employment dates and approved leave.
