Company Knowledge Base — Employee Benefits

Full-time employees are eligible for the company's health insurance plan after completing the applicable eligibility period. Coverage options and employee contributions are described in the current benefits enrollment materials.

Employees may participate in the retirement savings program subject to the plan's eligibility rules. Company matching contributions, when applicable, follow the plan terms.

Employees should review benefit elections during the annual enrollment window. Qualifying life events may allow changes outside the normal enrollment period.

HR can provide current plan documents and confirm an employee's individual eligibility. This knowledge-base document is a general guide and does not replace official plan documents.
