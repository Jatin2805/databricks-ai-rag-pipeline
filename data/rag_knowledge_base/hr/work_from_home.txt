Company Knowledge Base — Work From Home Policy

Eligible employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Teams may establish specific collaboration days when employees are expected to work from the office.

Employees working remotely must maintain normal working hours unless an alternative schedule has been approved. They must remain reachable through the company's approved communication tools during working hours.

Company laptops must be used for company work. Employees should avoid public Wi-Fi when handling confidential information; if public Wi-Fi is unavoidable, the company VPN must be used.

Remote work does not change an employee's responsibility to protect company information, attend required meetings, or meet agreed deadlines.
