Company Knowledge Base — Data Privacy Policy

Personal data should be collected only for legitimate business purposes and handled according to applicable privacy requirements.

Employees should access personal information only when their role requires it. Personal data should not be copied into personal devices, public documents, or unapproved external services.

When sharing information externally, employees must verify the recipient and confirm that the transfer is authorized.

Security incidents involving personal or confidential data should be reported promptly through the approved incident-response process. Employees should preserve relevant evidence and avoid making unauthorized changes to affected systems.
