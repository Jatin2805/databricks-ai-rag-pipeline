Company Knowledge Base — Password and Authentication Policy

Employees must use strong, unique passwords for company accounts and must not reuse company passwords on personal services.

Multi-factor authentication is required for access to company systems that support MFA.

Passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, and API secrets must never be shared through chat, email, tickets, or documents unless an approved secure-secret workflow specifically requires it.

If an employee believes credentials have been exposed, they should change the affected credential immediately and report the incident through the company's security process.
