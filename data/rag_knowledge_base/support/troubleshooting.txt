Company Knowledge Base — Troubleshooting Guide

If a user cannot sign in, first verify that the correct account email is being used and that the service is not reporting an outage. Users should not repeatedly guess passwords.

If dashboards load slowly, check the browser connection, refresh the session, and determine whether the issue affects one dashboard or the entire workspace. Large queries can also increase load time.

If scheduled reports are not delivered, verify that the schedule is enabled, the recipient addresses are correct, and the recipients still have permission to access the report.

If the issue continues, contact support with the approximate time of the problem, affected workspace, and relevant error message. Do not include passwords or secret tokens.
