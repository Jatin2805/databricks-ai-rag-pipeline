Company Knowledge Base — Cancellation Policy

Customers can request cancellation through the account billing area or by contacting the billing team.

Cancellation generally prevents the next renewal. The exact end date of access depends on the customer's subscription terms.

Customers should review any annual-contract or enterprise agreement before cancellation because contractual commitments may have different requirements.

Cancellation does not automatically imply a refund for previously paid service periods. Refund eligibility is determined under the applicable refund terms or contract.
