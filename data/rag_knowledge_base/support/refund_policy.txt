Company Knowledge Base — Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase, subject to the applicable terms of the purchased product or plan.

Refund requests should include the order or invoice reference and a short explanation of the request. Customers should never send passwords, authentication codes, or full payment-card details in a support ticket.

Refunds may be unavailable for certain promotional credits, consumed services, or purchases governed by separate contractual terms.

Approved refunds are returned using the original payment method when practical. Processing time can vary depending on the payment provider.
